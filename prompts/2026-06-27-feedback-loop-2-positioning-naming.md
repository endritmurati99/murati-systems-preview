# Feedback Loop 2 — Positioning, Naming, CTA

## Purpose

Use this context inside the ChatGPT project `Murati Digital` while the final brand name is still open. The page should position the business as a team-based IT systems service, not as a website-only studio.

## Owner Feedback

- The first viewport is strong, but lower sections feel too flat and hard to read.
- The business should not be understood as only building websites.
- The offer is closer to real IT services: digital systems, automations, internal tools, web presence, technical care.
- The website needs clearer repeated call-to-action moments.
- `Wer wir sind` should describe a team, not Endrit as a solo person.
- `Murati Systems` may not be the final name; naming should remain open.
- The top-left brand can be a small mark and does not need to expose the full name yet.
- Reduce abbreviations and vague labels.

## Research Basis

- NN/g: link and button labels should clearly set expectations for the click target, not use vague action labels.
  - https://www.nngroup.com/articles/better-link-labels/
  - https://www.nngroup.com/articles/get-started/
- NN/g: users read more when copy is clear, simple, and easy to understand.
  - https://www.nngroup.com/articles/legibility-readability-comprehension/
- W3C WCAG 2.2, Success Criterion 1.4.3: normal text needs sufficient contrast, with 4.5:1 as the Level AA baseline.
  - https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html
- Baymard homepage UX: a homepage should orient users quickly, communicate value, and guide the next step.
  - https://baymard.com/blog/ecommerce-homepage-ux

## Applied Direction

- Hero message: IT systems that win customers and organize work.
- Primary CTA: `Vorhaben prüfen lassen`.
- WhatsApp CTA: `Vorhaben per WhatsApp senden`.
- Team section replaces solo founder copy.
- Lower sections gain stronger contrast panels and repeated concrete CTAs.
