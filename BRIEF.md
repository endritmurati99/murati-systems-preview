# Brief — Murati Systems

> Phase-0 draft. This is the working brief for Endrit Murati's own IT/software
> service brand. No build has started yet.

## Required
- Project name: Murati Systems
- Sector: AI-native IT services, software, websites, automations, internal tools, technical management
- Primary goal / conversion: qualify serious project inquiries from DACH/international clients through a direct contact/strategy-call CTA
- Style axes: technical, premium, motion-led, modern, precise, founder-led; avoid generic "AI agency" visuals
- Pages: first version as a high-impact single-page site; later expand to services, cases, about, contact
- Tone / voice: confident, clear, technical but understandable; founder-led, direct, no agency fluff
- Languages: German first; structure should be ready for English/international version later

## Useful if known

  - Working title: Murati Systems. Previous ChatGPT project name: Murati Digital; final naming is still open.
  - Endrit Murati wants a personal but scalable brand. "Murati" should be visible.
  - "Murati Systems" currently feels stronger than "Murati Digital", but naming remains open.
  - The business should not be boxed into local web design. It should read as an AI-native IT/software studio.
  - Endrit uses AI agents heavily in his workflow: Claude/Codex-style coding agents, Hermes, McClure, and his own website-building agent/workflow.

  - Hero must instantly communicate that Murati builds modern IT systems, websites, software, and automations with an AI-native workflow.

  - Services should be framed as outcomes, not buzzwords:
    - Websites and landing pages
    - Business software and internal tools
    - Process automation and workflow integration
    - AI-assisted systems and agents

  - Include a "selected directions / proof" area:
    - Murati KFZ as a potential web/design proof item if approved later.

    - Integrate automation
    - Maintain and improve

- No-gos:
  - Do not position as just a local Dortmund/NRW web designer.
  - Do not lead with "we do AI magic" or generic "KI-Agentur" promises.
  - Do not overpromise revenue, efficiency, or automation outcomes.
  - Do not make the first site feel like a simple template.
  - Avoid fake enterprise scale. Founder-led is good; pretending to be a large agency is not.
  - Avoid public fixed pricing for complex software/automation work.
  - Avoid difficult/chaotic clients; copy should subtly filter for serious businesses.
- Contact / assets:
  - Real logo, brand colors, photo, and contact details are not finalized.

  - Production direction should assume Next.js + TypeScript + Tailwind.
  - Use 21st.dev/shadcn/Radix-style components only when they fit the visual system.
  - Motion should be performant, purposeful, and reduced-motion friendly.
  - Avoid heavy 3D unless it materially improves the first impression and passes mobile checks.
- Deadline / depth:
  - Current phase: Brief only.
  - Next phase after approval: generate 3 distinct single-file directions, then Gate 1 pick.

## Positioning Draft
Murati Systems builds websites, software, and automations for businesses that want sharper digital systems without agency overhead. The work is AI-native, founder-led, and technical from day one: fast landing pages, internal tools, workflow automation, and long-term technical management.

## Offer Architecture
- Entry offer: modern website / landing page for a small business or professional service provider.
- Core offer: website plus a practical business system, such as lead intake, documentation, CRM/Sheet workflows, email flows, or admin tooling.
- Higher-value offer: custom internal tool, workflow automation, or AI-assisted process system.
- Recurring offer: maintenance, hosting, monitoring, content updates, workflow support, and technical management.

## Website Direction
- First viewport should feel like a real technology operator, not a template:
  - bold brand wordmark
  - compact founder-led signal
  - motion/system visual in the background or alongside the hero
  - visible examples of work types: Websites, Software, Automation, AI Agents
  - one direct CTA
- New attached visual reference from Endrit:
  - cinematic black/space hero with a large glowing orbital/portal/black-hole form entering from the top.
  - sparse star/particle field, lots of black negative space, centered typography, minimal nav, single pill CTA.
  - emotional target: "innovation", "systems", "AI-native", "wow", high-end tech operator.
  - Use as visual DNA only; do not clone logo, copy, layout measurements, or exact artwork.
  - For Murati Systems, translate the image into a proprietary "system core / orbital intelligence" hero.
- Visual language:
  - dark or high-contrast technical base is allowed, but avoid generic purple/blue AI gradients.
  - Use precise typography, subtle motion, system diagrams, interface fragments, terminal/workflow signals, or layered product surfaces.
  - The site should feel modern enough that a visitor believes Endrit understands AI tooling deeply.
  - For the cinematic direction, prefer black, white, cold cyan, restrained steel/graphite, and one sharp accent. Avoid decorative blobs and stock AI-neon.
- Hero copy candidates for this direction:
  - "Systems for the AI-native era"
  - "KI-native Systeme fuer Unternehmen, die schneller bauen wollen"
  - "Websites, Software und Automationen - gebaut mit Agenten-Geschwindigkeit"
  - "Murati Systems builds modern websites, internal tools, and AI workflows for serious operators."
- Image / motion production route:
  - Static first: generated bitmap hero background, optimized and darkened with an overlay for readability.
  - Motion upgrade: separate particle/orbit/canvas layer with slow rotation, parallax, and reduced-motion fallback.
  - Tools: Gemini/Imagegen/Midjourney-style prompt for the background, then Next.js + Tailwind, optional Three.js/Canvas/GSAP for motion.
  - The image should be custom-made for Murati Systems, not a downloaded generic AI wallpaper.
- Hero references:
  - 21st.dev hero components for layout mechanics and React/Tailwind patterns.
  - MotionSites.ai for animated hero concepts.
  - Awwwards tech/AI references for craft and motion ambition.
  - User-attached "AI at the Pace of New Innovation" screenshot as a mood reference for cinematic composition.
- Do not copy references directly; synthesize into a restrained, high-signal Murati Systems direction.
